### Pre-Match Briefing: Wolverhampton Wanderers (Matchday 35)
**Date:** 26 April 2026  
**Author:** Julian Hawthorn (First-Team Analyst)  
**Audience:** First-Team Coaching Staff  

Wolverhampton Wanderers often drop into a back five when defending, prioritizing defensive solidity and rapid counter-attacks. They are comfortable conceding possession, maintaining a highly organized low block that restricts space between the lines.

Their defensive structure relies heavily on forcing opponents wide. They record a PPDA of 16.2, indicating a complete refusal to press high up the pitch. On attacking set-pieces, they rely heavily on short corner routines, utilizing three players around the ball to manipulate defensive structures before driving into the penalty area along the byline.

To break down their deep defensive block, I recommend utilizing our full-backs as inverted midfielders. This will draw their wide wing-backs out of position and create 1v1 scenarios for our wingers against their isolated outside centre-backs. Additionally, our midfielders must be prepared to commit immediate tactical fouls to prevent their forwards from breaking out in transition.
