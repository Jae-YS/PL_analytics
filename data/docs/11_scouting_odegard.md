### Scouting Report: Stefan Odegard (Fjordvik IF)
**Date:** 14 February 2026  
**Author:** Silas Vance (Senior Recruitment Analyst)  
**Target:** Stefan Odegard (Age: 24, Left-Back)  

Stefan Odegard has quietly assembled an outstanding statistical profile for Fjordvik IF in the Norwegian Eliteserien. Across 1,980 competitive minutes, the modern full-back averages a remarkable 8.10 progressive passes per 90. He is a high-volume crosser, attempting 5.40 deliveries per match with a 28.5% completion rate. Crucially, his defensive application is robust, winning 64.2% of his defensive duels in wide areas.

The primary concern regarding his transition to the Premier League is his vulnerability to aerial balls directed to the back post. Standing at 174 cm, he wins a mere 41.5% of his aerial battles, presenting a distinct mismatch against physical wide forwards.

Odegard's contract with Fjordvik expires in December 2027. His agent, Bjorn Lillevik, has communicated that a release clause of €3.8 million can be triggered by foreign clubs. I recommend we immediately activate this clause, as his statistical profile provides excellent value for a rotational full-back.
