### Scouting Report: Dariusz Leka (FC Dunavska Zora)
**Date:** 20 January 2025  
**Author:** Silas Vance (Senior Recruitment Analyst)  
**Target:** Dariusz Leka (Age: 23, Centre-Back)  

Dariusz Leka has established himself as a dominant defensive anchor for FC Dunavska Zora in the Bulgarian First League. Across 1,600 minutes played this season, Leka has exhibited tremendous aerial authority, winning 72.8% of his aerial duels. His ball-playing capabilities are functional, successfully executing 6.30 progressive passes per 90. Defensively, he averages a staggering 8.40 clearances per match, highlighting his proactive penalty box defending.

The most glaring flaw in his profile is his lack of agility when defending large spaces. His lateral turning radius is poor, and he struggles significantly when dragged out of the central corridor by mobile strikers dropping deep.

Leka's current contract expires in June 2028. Intermediary sources indicate FC Dunavska Zora are demanding a transfer fee in the region of €2.8 million. Considering his physical dominance and low acquisition cost, I recommend we secure a preliminary agreement for a summer transfer to bolster our squad depth.
