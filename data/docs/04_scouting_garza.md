### Scouting Report: Mateo Garza (Atlético Quillahue)
**Date:** 11 February 2024  
**Author:** Elena Rostova (South American Scout)  
**Target:** Mateo Garza (Age: 19, Right Winger)  

Mateo Garza has emerged as a high-potential wide forward for Atlético Quillahue in the Chilean Primera División. Having amassed 1,150 senior minutes, the teenager displays exceptional underlying attacking metrics. He generates 2.40 shots per 90, with an average shot quality of 0.15 xG per shot, culminating in a highly respectable 0.36 expected goals per 90. In addition, his carrying ability is elite, successfully completing 4.85 dribbles per match.

His main developmental bottleneck is his defensive work rate out of possession. He registers merely 0.65 defensive actions in his own half per 90, often leaving his right-back completely isolated during opposition counter-attacks. He requires significant tactical coaching to survive in a high-pressing Premier League system.

Garza's contract runs until December 2026. His representation, the Santiago-based agency Altavista Representaciones, claims a release clause exists at $3.2 million. We recommend immediate engagement with his agents to negotiate personal terms ahead of a summer transfer.
