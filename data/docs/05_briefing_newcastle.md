### Pre-Match Briefing: Newcastle United (Matchday 4)
**Date:** 10 September 2024  
**Author:** Julian Hawthorn (First-Team Analyst)  
**Audience:** First-Team Coaching Staff  

Newcastle United continue to deploy an intense, physical 4-3-3 system designed to suffocate opposition build-up. Their shape is incredibly compact vertically, relying heavily on the athleticism of their midfield trio to win second balls and sustain pressure in the attacking half.

Their build-up play is highly direct when under pressure. Their goalkeeper averages 12.4 long passes per match into the opposition half, actively avoiding short distribution to the centre-backs. When defending corners, Newcastle utilise a heavily populated zonal marking system, keeping 8 players inside the penalty area while leaving two rapid forwards on the edge of the box to initiate counter-attacks. 

We strongly recommend leaving an extra holding midfielder on the edge of the penalty arc during our offensive corners to nullify their rapid breakout threat. In possession, drawing their midfield press towards our left flank before playing quick horizontal switches will allow our right-winger 1v1 isolation against their left-back.
