# Synthetic documents

All 12 documents in this folder are **synthetic**: written for a demo and
reviewed by hand. They describe the analytics department of a fictional
Premier League club ("the club", never named). Every person, quote,
statistic, fee and event in them is invented.

## Purpose

They test retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over unstructured,
internal-style text. They are not real scouting reports, and nothing in
them should be treated as fact about any real player, manager or club.

## Timeline

Documents are dated from 18 August 2023 to 26 April 2026, four per
season for 2023/24, 2024/25 and 2025/26. This matches the seasons of
match data loaded into the database.

## What is real and what is invented

- **Scouting targets** play for invented clubs in real countries.
  All players, agents, scouts, fees, release clauses and contract
  terms are invented. Any resemblance to real people or organisations
  is coincidental.
- **Pre-match briefings** name real Premier League opponents so they
  can be paired with real match data. Every tactical tendency and
  pressing figure attributed to them is invented and may not match how
  those clubs actually played. Briefings contain no match results or
  statements about real players or managers.
- **Memos** describe the fictional club's internal processes.
- **Statistics** are illustrative and not taken from any real data
  provider.
