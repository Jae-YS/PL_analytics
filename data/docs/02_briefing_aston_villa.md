### Pre-Match Briefing: Aston Villa (Matchday 9)
**Date:** 20 October 2023  
**Author:** Marcus Quell (Lead Opposition Analyst)  
**Audience:** First-Team Coaching Staff  

Aston Villa often approach fixtures in a compact mid-to-high block, aggressively stepping up to compress the midfield. Their defensive line operates incredibly high, aiming to catch opponents offside while maintaining a narrow horizontal shape. 

Their pressing intensity is a key feature of their out-of-possession approach. They currently allow a PPDA of just 9.8, aggressively hunting the ball when it enters the middle third. Once possession is won, they transition immediately through vertical channels rather than recycling the ball deep. On offensive set-pieces, they heavily favour deep, out-swinging deliveries towards the back post, attempting to isolate their tallest centre-back against our full-backs.

To bypass their press, we recommend instructing our goalkeeper to bypass the midfield entirely with lofted passes into our wingers. Because their full-backs invert aggressively during their own build-up, immediate diagonal switches upon winning possession will expose massive vacant areas on the flanks.
