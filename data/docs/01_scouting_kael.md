### Scouting Report: Joren Kael (FC Kystborg)
**Date:** 18 August 2023  
**Author:** Silas Vance (Senior Recruitment Analyst)  
**Target:** Joren Kael (Age: 21, Defensive Midfielder)  

We have completed our preliminary data assessment of Joren Kael following his standout performances for FC Kystborg in the Danish Superliga. Across 1,420 league minutes over the past twelve months, Kael has established himself as a premier ball-winner. He averages 2.60 interceptions per 90, which ranks in the 94th percentile for his division. His distribution is equally reliable, completing 88.2% of his medium-range passes under pressure. Furthermore, he registers 7.15 progressive passes per 90, driving his team from deep areas.

His primary weakness is his lack of burst pace when defending lateral transitions. If isolated against agile wingers, his recovery speed leaves the backline exposed. Consequently, he often commits tactical fouls, resulting in a high card accumulation rate.

Kael's current contract expires in June 2027. FC Kystborg's sporting director, Mikkel Strandholt, has privately indicated an asking price of €5.4 million. Given our need for a deep-lying progressor, I strongly recommend we send a live scout to their next home fixture before submitting an initial €4.5 million bid.
