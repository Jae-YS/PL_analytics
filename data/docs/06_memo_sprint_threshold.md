### Tactical Memo: High-Intensity Sprint Threshold Update
**Date:** 08 November 2024  
**Author:** Callum Ashdown (Director of Sports Science)  
**Recipient:** Medical and Analytics Staff  

To align with modern European physical tracking standards, the department will modify the velocity thresholds used to classify high-intensity sprints. This update is necessary to ensure our physical profiling matches the proprietary data supplied by our new tracking vendor.

Previously, any movement exceeding 24.5 km/h was classified as a high-intensity sprint. Effective 15 November 2024, the baseline threshold for this classification will be elevated to 25.2 km/h. Additionally, the acceleration parameter for maximum exertion bursts will shift from 3.0 m/s² to 3.5 m/s². 

This calibration will inherently reduce the reported sprint distances for all players. Retrospective analysis shows that our squad's average high-intensity running volume per match will decrease from 1,850 metres to 1,620 metres under the new framework. All physical load alerts and injury prevention algorithms have been updated to reflect these adjusted baselines.
