### Tactical Memo: Match Day Roster Age Profiling
**Date:** 03 November 2025  
**Author:** Dr. Aris Thorne (Head of Data Science)  
**Recipient:** Sporting Director & Recruitment Staff  

To ensure long-term squad sustainability and future resale value, the board has approved a structural adjustment to our match day squad profiling guidelines. Historical analysis over the past three seasons indicates a severe drop in physical tracking output during the final 20 minutes when the average age of our midfield substitutes exceeds a specific threshold.

Previously, our internal squad building model mandated that the average age of the 20-man match day squad should not exceed 26.5 years. Effective 10 November 2025, this target threshold will be permanently lowered to 25.8 years. 

Furthermore, the percentage of match minutes allocated to players under the age of 23 must increase from a baseline of 18.5% to a mandatory 22.0% over the course of the season. The recruitment department must prioritize targeting U-21 talent in the upcoming January window to ensure we meet these newly established strategic objectives.
