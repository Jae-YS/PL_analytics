### Tactical Memo: Expected Threat (xT) Model Calibration
**Date:** 14 December 2023  
**Author:** Dr. Aris Thorne (Head of Data Science)  
**Recipient:** Performance Analysis Department  

Following a review of our final-third efficiency metrics, the analytics department is recalibrating the club's Expected Threat (xT) framework to better value cut-backs from the byline. Our previous model undervalued these actions, over-indexing on central penetration.

Historically, a successful pass from the half-spaces into the penalty area carried a baseline xT value of 0.12. However, internal tracking data reveals that wide cut-backs from within 15 yards of the goal line have yielded significantly higher quality shooting opportunities. Effective 20 December 2023, the base xT reward for a successful byline cut-back will be manually adjusted from 0.09 to 0.16 to reflect its true creative value.

Analysts must apply this patch to all post-match reporting dashboards. Consequently, historical shot-creating action numbers from August 2022 onwards will reflect a 4.3% positive variance for our wide players. Please ensure the coaching staff is aware of this valuation shift before the January transfer window preparations.
